# 🛍️ Customer Behavior Analysis

### Retail & E-Commerce Data Analytics Project
A data analytics project analyzing 3,900 retail customer transactions to uncover purchasing patterns, revenue drivers, customer loyalty insights, and marketing opportunities using SQL, Python, and Power BI.


## 📊 Project Overview
Modern retail companies collect large volumes of transactional data, but raw data alone does not provide business value. The goal of this project is to transform raw customer transaction data into actionable business insights.\
This project analyzes customer purchasing behavior, product performance, discount impact, subscription usage, and demographic revenue patterns.
<br/>
The analysis helps answer key business questions such as:
- Which customers generate the most revenue?
- Do discounts attract high-value customers or reduce profit?
- Do subscriptions increase customer spending?
- Which products perform best across categories?
- How does revenue vary across demographic groups?


## 🎯 Business Problem

Retail businesses often struggle with:
- Understanding customer spending behavior
- Evaluating discount effectiveness
- Identifying high-value customer segments
- Improving customer retention and loyalty
- Optimizing product performance
  
This project builds a data-driven framework to solve these problems using SQL analysis and interactive dashboards.

The project investigates 10 key business questions:

3️⃣ Top 5 products with the highest ratings

4️⃣ Purchase comparison between Standard vs Express shipping

5️⃣ Do subscribed customers spend more?

6️⃣ Products with the highest discount usage rate

<br/>
All questions were answered using PostgreSQL queries and business analysis.


## 📊 Key Insights

Customer Revenue
- Male customers generate 67.7% of total revenue
- Female customers spend slightly more per transaction

Discount Behavior
- 43% of purchases involve discounts
- Nearly 50% of discount users still spend above average
  
Customer Loyalty
- 79.9% of customers are loyal buyers
- Only 2.1% are new customers
  
Subscription Insights
- Subscribers do not spend more per transaction
- 72% of repeat buyers are not subscribed
  
Product Performance\
Top rated products include:
- Gloves
- Sandals
- Boots
- Hat
- Skirt

## 💡 Business Recommendations
Based on the analysis, the following strategies were proposed:
#### 1️⃣ Improve Subscription Program
Introduce incentives such as:
- Free express shipping
- Loyalty rewards
- Early product access

#### 2️⃣ Reduce Discount Dependency
Items like Hats and Sneakers rely heavily on discounts.

Suggested solution:
- Product bundling
- Controlled promotions

#### 3️⃣ Increase New Customer Acquisition
Only 2% of customers are new buyers, indicating weak acquisition efforts.

Recommended strategies:
- Referral programs
- Targeted digital marketing
- Younger demographic campaigns

#### 4️⃣ Expand Female Customer Targeting
Female customers have higher average purchase value.\
Opportunity for growth through targeted marketing.
